## 1.4 Refund & Cancellation Policy

### A. Instalment Plan Cancellation

Refund of accumulated instalments minus RM100/month admin fee (maximum RM300) and the cost of any activated VAS.

### B. Direct Purchase Cancellation

| Cancellation Timing | Refund Policy |
| --- | --- |
| More than 90 days before departure | Full refund minus RM300 administrative fee |
| Between 60 and 90 days before departure | 50% refund of deposit paid |
| Less than 30 days before departure | No refund |
| Cancellation by Travel Agency | Refund determined by agency's package terms |
